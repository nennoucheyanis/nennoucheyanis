PROJECT / 01

Eco Dôme
du Hamma

A graduation project proposing the redevelopment of the zoo area of Jardin d'Essai du Hamma in Algiers.

Inspired by the geometry of a snail shell, the project explores architecture as an ecosystem where geometry, climate, material and living systems interact.

Location
Jardin d'Essai du Hamma
Algiers, Algeria
Type
Public / Cultural
Area
1.2 hectares
Approach
Biomimicry / Environment / Computational Design
01

Concept

The architectural concept is derived from the spiral geometry of a snail shell. The shell becomes both a formal and environmental strategy, generating a continuous circulation sequence while creating controlled climatic conditions for different ecosystems.

02

Genesis

The project develops from an exploration of natural geometry, movement and environmental adaptation. The spiral becomes a generative system from which the building form, circulation, spatial sequence and relationship between the different ecosystems are progressively developed.

Materials

Material selection is considered as part of the environmental performance of the Eco Dôme. The envelope and construction materials are selected according to their thermal behaviour, transparency, adaptability and relationship with the controlled ecosystems inside the dome.

01 / ETFE envelope

Lightweight and highly transparent envelope used to create the climatic interface of the dome while allowing controlled solar transmission and natural daylight.

03 / Diaphragm Opening

A kinetic ventilation opening inspired by the mechanical logic of an iris diaphragm. Nine overlapping blades pivot around eccentric points to continuously modify the central aperture. When the system opens, the increased free area facilitates air movement; when it closes, the aperture is reduced to limit direct exposure and airflow.

09

Hygrothermal
simulation

The environmental strategies are finally tested through hygrothermal simulation. The analysis is developed at two different scales: first, through a material-based comparison using WUFI, and then through an AI-assisted assessment of the complete envelope geometry.

01
WUFI / Material comparison

Three envelope materials are evaluated under the same climatic conditions: single glazing, metal and ETFE. The simulations compare their thermal behaviour throughout the year and during extreme hot and cold periods.

02
AI-assisted / Full-envelope simulation

After identifying the most relevant material behaviours at the material scale, the analysis is extended to the complete envelope geometry.

METHODOLOGY
Due to the complexity of the complete geometry and the computational time required for a full hygrothermal simulation, an AI-assisted model was used to estimate the temperature and relative humidity behaviour of the complete envelope.

The model provides a comparative overview of the three material configurations before further design development and validation.
01
Temperature

Comparative temperature evolution of the three envelope materials across the complete simulated period.

09 / Conclusion
The comparative analysis indicates that ETFE provides the most favourable overall thermal and hygrothermal response among the three investigated envelope configurations.
