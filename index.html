My approach combines parametric modelling, environmental simulation, biomimicry and computational design to investigate how architecture can respond to its context.

01

Eco Dôme
du Hamma

Location Algiers
Site 1.2 ha
Type Public
Year 2026

A biomimetic architectural system developed for the redevelopment of the zoo area of the Jardin d'Essai du Hamma.

Inspired by the geometry of a snail shell, the project creates a controlled environment where architecture, vegetation, climate and animal habitats coexist as one ecosystem.

Project view · 01
01 / Concept

Architecture
as an ecosystem.

The project investigates the relationship between architecture and living systems. The snail shell becomes a geometric generator: a continuous spatial sequence wrapping around different climatic environments.
