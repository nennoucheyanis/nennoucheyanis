Architecture
through computation.

I am an architect developing an approach at the intersection of architecture, environmental performance and computational design. My work explores how digital tools can help generate, analyse and optimise architectural systems rather than simply represent them.

From parametric modelling to environmental simulation, I use computation as an extension of the design process, with a focus on climate, geometry and performance.
