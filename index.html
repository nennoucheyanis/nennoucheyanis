PROJECT / 01

Eco Dôme
du Hamma

A biomimetic architectural system developed for the redevelopment of the zoo area of Jardin d'Essai du Hamma, Algiers.

Inspired by the geometry of a snail shell, the project explores the relationship between architecture, ecosystems, climate and environmental performance.

Concept

The architectural concept is derived from the spiral geometry of a snail shell. The shell becomes a spatial and environmental strategy, generating a continuous circulation sequence while creating controlled climatic conditions for different ecosystems.

About
Yanis Nennouche Architect
Architecture through computation.

I am an architect working at the intersection of architecture, environmental performance and computational design.

My approach explores how digital tools can become part of the design process itself: generating geometry, analysing performance and developing architectural systems.

I am particularly interested in biomimicry, parametric design, environmental simulation and generative approaches to architecture.
